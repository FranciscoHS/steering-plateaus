\subsection*{Pair objective with a monotonicity (inside-the-box) penalty}

We optimize a pair of unit directions $(d_1,d_2)$ jointly. Per anchor $a$ and
perturbation angle $\alpha$, the norm-matched perturbations along $d_1$, $d_2$,
and their equal mix give downstream $L^2$ responses $R_1(\alpha)$, $R_2(\alpha)$,
$R_{\mathrm{mix}}(\alpha)$.

\paragraph{Reward (unchanged).} Push the mix into the plateau relative to the
axes:
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ratio}}
= \big\langle\, R_{\mathrm{mix}}(\alpha)\,/\,(R_1(\alpha)+R_2(\alpha)) \,\big\rangle_{a,\alpha}.
\]

\paragraph{Monotonicity penalty (new).} The model
$R=F\!\big(\sum_i w_i|\hat f_i\!\cdot v|^p\big)$ is a sum of non-negative terms,
so adding a component cannot reduce the response: every iso-response point lies
\emph{at or inside the square}. Equivalently, the mix at $\alpha$ has the same
projection onto $d_1$ as a pure-$d_1$ perturbation at $\alpha'$ with
\[
\sin\alpha' = \sin\alpha\,\cos\tfrac{\pi}{4} = \sin\alpha/\sqrt2,
\]
plus an extra $d_2$ component, hence the model requires
\[
R_{\mathrm{mix}}(\alpha) \;\ge\; \max\big(R_1(\alpha'),\,R_2(\alpha')\big).
\]
Cancellation (points \emph{outside} the box) is the only way to violate this.
Penalize the dimensionless shortfall:
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{box}}
= \Big\langle\, \mathrm{ReLU}\!\Big(1 - \frac{R_{\mathrm{mix}}(\alpha)}{\max\big(R_1(\alpha'),R_2(\alpha')\big)}\Big) \,\Big\rangle_{a,\alpha}.
\]
It is $0$ everywhere at/inside the box and positive only for cancellation.

\paragraph{Orthogonality (unchanged).}
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{orth}} = (\hat d_1\!\cdot\hat d_2)^2$.

\paragraph{Total.}
\[
\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ratio}}
+ \lambda_{\mathrm{box}}\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{box}}
+ \lambda_{\mathrm{orth}}\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{orth}}.
\]
Minimizing this still rewards a plateau-extending mix, but the optimizer can no
longer reach $R_{\mathrm{mix}}\!\approx\!0$ by walking outside the model
(cancellation); the lowest legitimate value sits at the box ($p\to\infty$).
