If we set $$n = 2$$ the response reduces to $$R(v) \;=\; F\!\left(\sum_i w_i (d_i^\top v)^2\right) \;=\; F(v^\top A v), \qquad A = \sum_i w_i\, d_i d_i^\top,$$ depending on $$v$$ only through the scalar $$v^\top A v$$.

This sensitivity function is fully determined by the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $$A$$: $$D$$ eigenvalues and an eigenbasis in $$D$$ dimensions. 

Under superposition, $$M > D$$ features span $$\mathbb{R}^D$$. For error correction to privilege feature directions, letting perturbations along them through while suppressing perturbations along non-feature directions, the sensitivity function would have to elevate every one of the $$M$$ features above non-feature directions. A quadratic form with only $$D$$ eigen-degrees-of-freedom cannot do this. Either $$A \propto I,$$ in which case every direction is equally sensitive and there is nothing to error-correct, or $$A$$ has direction-dependent sensitivity with at most $$D$$ principal axes, in which case some features are indistinguishable from non-features in sensitivity. Our observed $$n \approx 2$$ therefore rules out any error-correcting scheme that privileges feature directions under superposition.
